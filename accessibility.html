Accessibility

How we make kidodino.com usable for everyone.

We want every parent and caregiver to be able to use kidodino.com, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard instead of a mouse, screen magnification, or larger text.

Standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, the most widely used standard for web accessibility.

What we have done

  • Every page and game works with a keyboard alone, and the keyboard focus is always clearly visible.
  • A "Skip to main content" link is the first thing on every page that has text to read.
  • Pages use real headings, landmarks and lists, so screen readers can move around easily.
  • Every meaningful image has a text description. Decorative shapes are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and background colors meet the WCAG AA contrast ratio.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200% without losing content, and the layout adapts to small screens.
  • Children don't need to read to play, and the games have no timers. Nothing in them needs dragging: every move can be made with a single tap, a click or the keyboard.
  • Movement is gentle and is shortened when your device asks for reduced motion.

Known limitations

The free coloring pages are pictures meant to be printed and colored. Because they are drawings, a screen reader cannot read them in full. If you would like a description of a page, write to us and we will send one.

In Kido's Treasure Hunt, the "by color" rounds ask children to tell colors apart. Children who find that hard can switch the same game to "by shape" with the switch beside the game, and find things by their shape instead.

We test with keyboard, screen magnification and automated checks. We have not yet had a formal audit by an outside expert.

Accessibility contact

If something on the site is hard to use, or you need information in another format, please write to kido@kidodino.com. We will reply as soon as we can.
