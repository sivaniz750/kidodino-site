Made for little hands

Every page is planned for children aged 3 to 6, so coloring ends with a finished picture and a proud smile.

  • Big, clear picturesThick lines and large shapes that a 3-year-old can fill, with room for a 6-year-old to add ideas.
  • A line to read togetherEvery picture has a short caption, like "Kido reads a book."
  • One picture per sheetThe back of every picture is blank, so markers never spoil the next page.
  • A friend to grow withKido is curious, kind and never scary, in every book and every game.

Meet Kido

Kido is a little green dino who learns by doing: he builds, he plays, he helps at home and he dreams about what he will be one day. Children color his adventures, then play with him here.

Did your child color a Kido page? Kido would love to see it. Send a photo of the picture to kido@kidodino.com, without your child's name or face.
