Play & learn with Kido

Short games where your child thinks and chooses, not just taps. Each one practises a skill that child-development research links to school readiness: shapes and space, flexible thinking, feelings and self-control. Kido reads every step aloud, so no reading is needed. Best played together.

  • Shapes · early math

    Kido Builds

    Choose the shape that fits each gap and build a house, a truck and a rocket. Big builders turn the shapes too.

    Play Kido Builds
  • Flexible thinking

    Sorting Switch

    Sort Kido's toys by color… then the rule changes! Children practise switching rules, a key thinking skill.

    Play Sorting Switch
  • Feelings · kindness

    Kido's Feelings

    Find out how Kido's friends feel, then choose what could help. Ends with a calm breath together.

    Play Kido's Feelings
  • Self-control · movement

    Kido Says: Freeze!

    Tap on green, freeze on red, and on opposite day it flips. Or stand up and freeze-dance with a grown-up.

    Play Kido Says: Freeze!

Made for little hands

Kido is curious, kind and always happy to try something new. No scary faces, no tricky details. Just a friend your child can color with, page after page.

  • Big pictures, thick linesEasy to color inside for a 3-year-old, with room for a 6-year-old to add their own ideas.
  • Words to read togetherEvery picture has a short line, like "Kido digs a hole." New words, first reading.
  • No bleed-throughEvery picture has a blank back, so markers never spoil the next page.
  • Quiet, screen-free timeFifty pictures for rainy afternoons, car rides and calm moments before bed.
