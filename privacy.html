Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

KidoDino coloring books and kidodino.com are made by Sivan Studio. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to information when you visit kidodino.com.

The short version

We do not collect personal information from anyone, children or adults. The site has no sign-up, no accounts, no forms, no ads, no analytics and no cookies.

What stays on your device

The games remember two choices, sound on or off and the game level, in your browser's storage on your own device. The site also remembers, until you close the tab, that it already showed you the free coloring pages window. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Our web host

Like every website, kidodino.com is delivered by a hosting service, GitHub Pages. To deliver the pages, GitHub receives standard technical details such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep them in its logs for security. We do not receive or use this information. GitHub's Privacy Statement explains how it handles it.

Everything else the site uses, including the fonts, pictures and games, is served from kidodino.com itself. We do not load anything from advertising or tracking services.

Links to Amazon

The "Buy on Amazon" buttons take you to Amazon. What happens there is covered by Amazon's own privacy notice. We do not receive any information about you from those clicks.

Children

Our books and games are made for children ages 3 to 6. Children never need to type anything on this site, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us personal information, please write to us and we will delete it.

Pictures you send us

If you email us a photo of a colored page, we use it only to reply to you, and we never post it anywhere without asking you first. Please send only the page, without a child's name or face.

Do Not Track

Because we do not track visitors, the site works the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Changes and contact

If anything here changes, we will update this page and the date below. Questions: kido@kidodino.com.
