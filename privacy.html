Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

KidoDino makes coloring books for young children. This page explains what information we collect on kidodino.com and what we do with it.

What we collect

Nothing. This website has no sign-up, no accounts and no forms, and it does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The games run only in your browser and save nothing.

Children

Our books and games are for children, and this website is designed so that children never need to type in any information. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

Pictures you send us

If you email us a photo of a colored page, we use it only to reply to you. We never post it anywhere without asking you first. Please send only the page, without a child's name or face.

Fonts

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to download them. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
