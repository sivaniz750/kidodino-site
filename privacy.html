Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

KidoDino makes coloring books for young children. This page explains what information we collect on kidodino.com and what we do with it.

What we collect

We collect only the email address of a parent or guardian who signs up to receive free coloring pages and book news. We also record that the person confirmed they are a parent or guardian, 18 or older.

Children

Our books are for children, but this website is for their parents. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their email address, write to us and we will delete it.

How we use your email

  • To send you free coloring pages and news about KidoDino books.
  • We never sell or share your email address with anyone for their own marketing.
  • Your email is stored with our email service provider, which sends the emails for us.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also write to kido@kidodino.com to see or delete the information we hold about you.

Fonts

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to download them. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
